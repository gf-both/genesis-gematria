Torah · Topology of Gematria — offline build

FILES
  index.html      the app (open by double-clicking; works fully offline)
  torah_data.js   Hebrew consonantal text of the 5 books (window.TORAH)
  torah_en.js     English KJV, verse-aligned (window.TORAH_EN)
  torah_gloss.js  per-word English gloss, transliteration, Strong's root,
                  part of speech, morphology (window.TORAH_GLOSS)
  three.min.js    Three.js r128 (local 3D engine)

FEATURES
  Layouts: Terrain / Scroll / Sphere / Meaning (3D) / Map (top-down)
  Value: per-letter or per-word gematria · books toggle · Stack/String
  Hover a word (Per-word mode) -> Hebrew, transliteration, English gloss,
    morphology, gematria.
  Double-click a word -> focus card: same-root highlight, same-gematria
    highlight, find-in-text (reader), clear.
  Search by reference (Genesis 1:1) or by meaning (king, light, covenant).
  Colour by gematria or by part of speech (with legend).
  Fly-through tour: fly-by or deep-dive, adjustable speed.
  Share link (encodes the current view in the URL) · Export .obj.

To change logic, edit the <script> at the bottom of index.html.
